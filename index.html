HypeClipper

The clip tool behind the HypeClipped YouTube channel.

HypeClipper is a private tool run by the owner of the HypeClipped YouTube channel. It finds the biggest moments in public live streams, cuts them into short vertical clips, and, once the channel owner approves each one, posts it to the HypeClipped channel. Every clip credits the streamer and links to their channel.

HypeClipped is a fan channel and is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or with the streamers it features.

Signing in with Google

HypeClipper asks for permission to upload videos to the HypeClipped channel, to read that channel's videos and their view counts, and to add videos to the channel's playlists. Only the channel owner signs in. HypeClipper is not offered to anyone else.
